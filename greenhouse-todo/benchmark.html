01 / Scenario

5 s visible warmup covers one whole route. Both routes move at 8 m/s with 0.55 s turns and 1.6 m eye height. Aisle: 15 s loop, task inspections within |x| ≤ 1.75 m. Forest: 20 s loop along all four walls at |x| ≤ 6.7 m, including a canopy inspection.

The app’s ?benchmark=1 mode owns storage isolation. This harness never reads or writes saved tasks and never starts audio.

Method & limits

Native requestAnimationFrame timestamps from the iframe measure actual callback intervals, including long stalls. Warmup and hidden intervals are excluded. Returning to a visible iframe starts a fresh 5 s warmup and a new sample segment. Rolling windows never bridge those gaps.

Percentiles use nearest rank. The 1% low is 1000 / p99, not the mean of the slowest 1%. Rolling FPS counts callbacks in every full one-second window, including windows immediately before a callback after a stall. Partial runs remain partial.

CPU, GPU, calls and triangles are the latest optional app frameMetrics snapshots; absent values stay null. GPU timings can lag. No timing-query, renderer, fetch or XHR interception is installed. Random scene animation and hardware load can still differ between runs. Screenshot buttons hold camera and sun, not animation time.
