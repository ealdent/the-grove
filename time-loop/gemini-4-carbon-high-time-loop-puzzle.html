The Boxwood Understudies

Winter fever and the levy took Céleste, Bastian, and young Noé from your benches, yet at first light the Guild Syndics inspect the Grand Seal Commission — three workshop halls built to be worked by five pairs of hands at once.

Strap on the brass Scribing Harness. Every stride you take across forty beats of the overhead Jacquard Drum punches a Manila card-lace to hang inside one of four carved Jura-boxwood understudies waiting on the iron rack.

Mind your own chalk: a boxwood rig marches blind by porcelain pawl and load-notch. Rob it of the iron it heaved, drop a grate across its shins, or cross its footing, and the Drum seizes dead.

Dawn at Rue Saint-Polycarpe · All Three Organs Seated

Five Hands Upon One Bench

Grey November light pales the high skylight and the tallow cressets gutter low. Across the chalked terracotta stand Céleste's narrow-shouldered rig, Bastian's heavy foundry frame, and young Noé's spare armature — motionless where their fortieth cards left them, brass knees warm from the night's work.

Inside the completed Grand Seal chassis, the crimson leather bellows draw a slow breath; the gilt songbird lifts its head, beats articulated wings, and pipes five clear notes into the rafters just as the Syndics' iron knocker sounds below. You unbuckle the Scribing Harness, hang the three punched Manila laces upon three empty apprentice pegs, and chalk the threshold: Measured true — nobody worked alone tonight.
