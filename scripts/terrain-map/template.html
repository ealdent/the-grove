Elevation USGS 3DEP bare-earth model, sampled at 40 ft cells for the map and 450 ft cells for the far horizon. Earth curvature and standard refraction are applied to sightlines.

Boundaries County GIS via NC OneMap. Roads, river, peaks OpenStreetMap. Forest ownership USDA Forest Service. Lakes are read from the hydro-flattened elevation surface.

Caveat Visibility ignores trees and buildings. The tract is wooded, so actual sightlines depend on what is cleared at a building site.
