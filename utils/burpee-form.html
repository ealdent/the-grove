Correct form

  • Squat down with the chest up; plant the palms flat just in front of the feet.
  • Kick both feet back to a plank — one straight line from head to heels, core braced.
  • Lower the chest to the floor, elbows tracking about 45° from the torso. No hip sag.
  • Press back to plank without letting the hips pike or droop.
  • Jump both feet forward, landing flat beside the hands.
  • Drive through the heels into a full jump — hips fully extended, arms overhead.
  • Land softly with bent knees, ready for the next rep.
