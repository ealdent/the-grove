About this experiment
Every sortie here is an AI-generated rail shooter, built to compare frontier models head-to-head on the same genre. Most are single one-shot generations with at most a nudge or two afterward — Redline Ascent is the exception, a tag-team build by multiple models. Each entry gets a light post pass for responsive / mobile layout and site framing. The badge in the corner of each card shows which model and effort level flew it.
