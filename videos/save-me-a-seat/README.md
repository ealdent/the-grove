# Save Me a Seat, hand-drawn

A music video for Jason's song "Save Me a Seat", 1920×1080 at 24 fps, 3:21. Every
frame is drawn by code on a 2D canvas, the way the "I animated this myself" videos
are made: no AI video and no 3D.

- Lines are inked with a wobble that redraws 12 times a second, so they boil like
  hand-drawn animation on twos.
- Colour is watercolour, printed a little off-register, on paper that changes sheet
  with every drawing.
- Memories are crayoned in, inside a hand-ruled frame on the page. The bridge is
  bare pencil.

The video follows the song line by line. Each line gets a shot cut on the beat grid,
56 in all, and the lyrics never appear on screen. Three rules hold throughout:

- **Colour.** The one strong colour is her yellow raincoat.
- **The lover.** She is never shown face-on: her face stays in the shadow of her
  hood, with red curls spilling out.
- **The narrator.** A small girl with a dark bob and a blue sweater. The jokes take
  the song's images literally.

## Watching it

`index.html` is the watch page: the 720p cut (`media/save-me-a-seat-720p.mp4`,
36 MB) with the words beside it. The current line is highlighted as it's sung, and
the voicemail lights up word by word on the intro. The night sky above the title
is drawn twelve times a second too; pull the moon's chain.

The lyrics went public with this page, at Jason's request (2026-10-18).

## Not in git

- `data.js` is the beat grid, loudness and the voicemail's word times.
- `out/` and `stills/` hold renders. The full-size renders (`*.mp4` outside
  `media/`) and `prof*/` are ignored too.
- `treatment.local.md` is the working shot list.

They come from `tmp/save-me-a-seat/` (the mix, the voicemail, the beat and lyric
analysis), which is gitignored at the repo root.

## Building it

```bash
python3 analyze.py                                  # data.js from the song (needs tmp/save-me-a-seat)
node render.mjs                                     # the whole song -> out/handdrawn.mp4
node render.mjs --from 30 --to 70 --out seg.mp4     # a segment
node render.mjs --stills 21.0,28.4,200.8            # PNG stills into stills/
python3 ../../tmp/save-me-a-seat/scripts/flash_check.py out/handdrawn.mp4
```

Chrome runs outside the command sandbox; `SONG=path` overrides the audio. The whole
song renders in about 90 s through `utils/degauss/video/cdp.mjs` into ffmpeg, tagged
BT.709. Open `video.html?t=SECONDS` in a browser to see one drawing.

## Files

- `ink.js`: lines, paint, paper, the camera.
- `cast.js`: the cartoon person: four views, rubber-hose limbs, walk and run cycles, faces.
- `cast2.js`: guests from every era, their hats, and a T. rex in a party hat.
- `props.js`, `props2.js`, `props3.js`: everything else, from the answering machine
  and the folding chairs to the torn page edge and the raincoat on its chair.
- `scenes.js` (0:00–0:30), `scenes_b.js` (to 1:10), `scenes_c.js` (to 2:11),
  `scenes_d.js` (to the end); `edit.js` puts the shots in order.
- `video.html` is the drawing page the renderer opens; `render.mjs` is the renderer.
- `probe_arm.mjs` logs which way each elbow bends, drawing by drawing.
- `probe_mem.mjs` watches the paint-texture cache.
- `probe_cuts.mjs` prints the cut times.
