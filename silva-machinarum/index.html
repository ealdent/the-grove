About this collection

Every story here answers the same brief, handed to one model after another: a short story about a woman who cannot die, living unnoticed on a generation ship among people who can, on the way to a world that looks less able to hold them the closer it comes.

The brief never changes, so the stories can be read against each other: the same ship, the same secret, and each model’s own idea of what she would do with it. The tag on a leaf names the model that wrote it and the effort level it ran at. Each leaf is catalogued the old way, by its opening words.
