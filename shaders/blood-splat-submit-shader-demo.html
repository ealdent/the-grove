Blood Splat Submit

Click the submit button and it stays down, bleeding. Blood floods its face with a slow fluid churn while the splatter bursts outward and drips a little way down the glass. Everything dries together — red settling into reddish-brown — then the dried blood flakes off in a breeze until only the spent, disabled button remains.
