Blood Splat Submit

An ordinary web form with an unfortunate submit handler. The button is a plain HTML <button>; the blood is a WebGL overlay rendered behind it. Click Submit and it takes a hit like a bug on a windshield — blood squeezes out around the button, spines and droplets fly, the face gets coated and drains dry — then the whole stain flakes away to dust. Every dial on the right is live.
