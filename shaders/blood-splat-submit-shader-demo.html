Blood Splat Submit

An ordinary web form with an unfortunate submit handler. Click the button and it bursts into medium-velocity impact spatter — a scalloped parent stain, radiating spines, satellite droplets, mist — then dries, flakes off the page, and stays disabled until you bring it back. Every dial on the right is live.
