Blood Splat Submit

An ordinary web form with an unfortunate submit handler. The button is a plain HTML <button>; the blood is a WebGL overlay. Click Submit and it takes a medium-velocity impact — scalloped parent stain, radiating spines, satellite droplets, mist — then dries, flakes off the page, and stays disabled until you bring it back.
