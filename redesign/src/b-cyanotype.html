The Grove

A site plan of every work on the ground: one mark per work, each plot drawn to its holdings, the fresh ink printing brighter than the old.

      About this direction

      A cyanotype site plan, after Anna Atkins's 1843 impressions of pressed specimens: every work is one mark, every section a plot drawn to its holdings, and the four benchmark plots are parcelled by the hand that made each work. The plots are laid out by a squarified treemap computed from the manifest, so the plan redraws itself whenever a work is added. Fresh marks print brighter than the first impression; the dotted line is the surveyor's walk through the last three weeks of additions, and the revision table in the title block is the changelog. The legend at left is the plain index for anyone who would rather read than wander.
