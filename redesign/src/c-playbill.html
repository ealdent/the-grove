    About this direction

    A playbill orders by importance rather than by folder: tonight's bill, the same piece in four companies, the season so far, and the full repertoire as cast lists. Type size is the hierarchy, as it was on a nineteenth-century bill where the star's name was the largest line and the cast the smallest. The bill and the four performances are drawn with the date, so the front of house is never the same two nights running; the plates print in one ink until you touch them. The company at the foot is the list of every model that has performed here, and choosing one lights its pieces in the repertoire.
