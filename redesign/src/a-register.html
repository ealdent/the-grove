About this direction

A register makes the collection's real structure the navigation: four briefs, forty hands, and the blanks where a brief has not yet been set. Nothing hides behind a category door. The paper is lit from wherever your pointer rests; hover an entry and its plate appears clipped to the desk, so you see a work before you open it. The rows and columns light together without any script, and the footings are summed live from the same manifest that would generate the README.

Made with curiosity & Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, and friends.
