To our patrons. Every variety in this list was raised on the ground from a written brief. Four of those briefs were sown, word for word, in the soil of every frontier model that came through the gate, and the trials that follow show what came up in each. The remainder is the nursery's own stock: implements, books, glasshouses and amusements for the long evenings.

Descriptions are honest. Where a variety was raised in one sowing we say so; where it was staked and pruned afterwards to stand up on a small screen, we say that too. Nothing here costs anything but the evening.

About this direction

A seedsmen's annual catalogue, in the manner of the 1890s trade lists with their chromolithograph plates and three-column descriptions. The Grove is a nursery; the four benchmark briefs are variety trials, one seed sown in forty soils, so every model's answer stands beside every other's on the same page with its grower named. Every variety carries a permanent catalogue number, the novelties are whatever came up most recently, and the proprietor's selection is drawn with the date. Density is the design: a catalogue is meant to be read slowly and returned to.
