About this direction

The Grove as a teletext service, set in the site's own Phosphor Wake pixel face. Page 100 is the index; every work has a permanent three-digit page you can type, link to, or reach with the coloured keys, and the four benchmark briefs sit on the four coloured buttons. A section rolls through its subpages the way a listings page did, the index carousel changes what is showing, and REVEAL turns each work's title screen into a forty-column mosaic in the seven teletext colours before you open the real thing. Nothing here is a picture of a screen: every row is text you can select, and every page number is a link.
