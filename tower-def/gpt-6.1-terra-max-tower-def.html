A brief history of tomorrow.

Once, the Bureau let everyone choose the weather for their most important days. People chose sunshine. The Bureau promised the rain could come tomorrow, and then tomorrow, and then tomorrow again.

All those postponed clouds waited together beyond the horizon. Now they have arrived to collect the last jar of dawn. The weather isn’t wicked; it has simply been waiting a very long time. Your job is to send it gently home before the jar runs out.

01 · Make a station

Select an instrument, then place it on a pale square. Instruments reach across the route; their circle shows their range.

02 · Read the forecast

Start a wave when you’re ready. Different weather moves at different speeds and costs different amounts of dawn if it gets through. Later forecasts are stronger.

03 · Mend & improve

Between forecasts, tap an instrument for four successive upgrades or sell it for 70% of your investment. Returned weather earns dew.

04 · Keep the light

Survive all 24 forecasts. The route is newly drawn for every sky. Use the speed and pause controls whenever you like.

Meet the weather

Late Drops are steady. Gustlings are fast. Pocket Fog is sturdier. Hail Parcels are armored and cost 2 dawn; Thunderheads cost 3. The slow, formidable Collector costs 5. Hover over a forecast’s weather for its current strength.

Keyboard: Tab to the field, move with the arrow keys, and press Enter to place or select. Escape clears your selection.
