📖 How to play
  • Bugs march from the log to your picnic basket. Don't let them steal your 🍪 snacks!
  • Buy towers in the shop, then tap a grassy spot to place them.
  • Tap a tower any time to upgrade (4 upgrades each) or sell it (70% back).
  • 🐌 Snail shells block 3 damage per hit — but light beams melt right through.
  • 🕷️ Spiders split into speedy mites. 🍯 Honeypots pay coins after each wave.
  • Keys: 1–5 pick towers, Space starts the wave, Esc cancels.
Every map is randomly generated — hit “New Map” for a fresh trail. 🐜🐝🪲🐌🕷️
