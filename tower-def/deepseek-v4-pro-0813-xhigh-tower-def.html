Ink & Ash

The Last Codex

The Great Library of Lumen kept one treasure above all others: the Codex Anima, a living book whose pages held every story ever told. Not ink on paper, but memory made manifest. As long as the Codex remembered, the world remembered — the name of a first love, the recipe for bread, the exact blue of a certain river, the shape of a friend's laughter.

Then came the Redaction. No one can say whether it is a censor-god, a glitch in the world, or simply the universe's long sigh of forgetting. It sends its servants to un-write existence: Scribbles that smear the ink of ordinary days, Smudges thick as regret, Censors that strike whole names with black bars, White-Outs that erase the small tender moments, and Plot Holes that swallow causes and their effects.

You are the last Archivist. The Scriptorium — a circle of enchanted desks and instruments — is all that stands between the Codex and total erasure. Draw your towers. Hold the margin. Do not let the last story end.
