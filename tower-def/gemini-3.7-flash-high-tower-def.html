📜 The Resonance Loom of Epsilon-7
"Before the celestial seas dried, the Primordial Fruiting Body was sown. A trillion hyper-conductive mycelial threads weaving through the mantle of Asteroid Epsilon-7, vibrating in perfect harmony to hold the atmospheric seal."

In Year 412 of the Spore-Age, deep tectonic fissures tore through the crust. From the silent void emerged the Dissonance Swarm: calcified horrors, glitch-mites, and phase-wraiths driven mad by harmonic silence.

As the Master Weaver of Myco-Acoustics, you must germinate resonant fungal towers along the magnetic spore-trails. Tune their sonic frequencies, lob explosive spore-pods, snare foes in honeyed amber, focus prismatic lasers, and unleash bio-electric tempest arcs before the swarm breaches the Heart-Spore.

Strategic Weaver Directives:

  • Preparation Phase: In between waves, study the incoming threat and upgrade key defensive choke-points.
  • 4 Upgrades per Spore: Each fungal tower evolves through 4 distinct visual and mechanical tiers.
  • Synergistic Elements: Goo-Hyphae slime renders enemies vulnerable to bonus damage; Tesla-Chanter shocks can chain through clustered swarms.
