The Typographer's Field Ledger (1888)

The Imperial Archive & The Obscurantist Rot

Beneath Alexandria-on-Thames, the steam-engine press Codex Aegis prints humanity's final archive upon endless vellum ribbons. But from the mouldering gutters rises the Obscurantist Rot—malevolent entities born of sour iron gall ink, redacted dossiers, and lost punctuation. They seek to taint the Master Matrix.

The Foundry Blueprints (5 Brass Mechanisms)

  • Hot-Lead Typecaster: Rapid kinetic linotype chamber spitting molten metal characters (!, ?, &, Q). Excellent against single skirmishers.
  • Vitriol Illuminator: Pressurized siphon spraying acidic gall-nut ink and gum arabic. Slows passing errata and coats the vellum in caustic puddles.
  • The Gilded Platen: Massive hydraulic stamper that strikes the parchment with concussive shockwaves, knocking back swarm units and crumbling armor.
  • Optic Prism Loupe: Ruby-ground solar telescope concentrating ambient light into piercing solar lances. Incredible range and armor penetration.
  • Bookbinder's Coil: Spinning copper bobbins launching electrified bookbinding thread that arcs between errata and tethers them together.

Erratum Classification

  • Typo-Mite: Nimble comma-bugs that scurry in rapid packs.
  • Ink-Blot: Squishy liquid blots that split into two Micro-Blots upon death.
  • Redaction Bar: Heavy black censor slabs that soak projectile damage.
  • Ampersand Gorgon: Slithering serpentine glyphs that surge in quick bursts.
  • Dog-Ear Golem: Hulking folded-parchment brutes resistant to blunt force.
  • The Censor's Shears (Miniboss): Snapping iron blades with sudden forward charges.
  • The Palimpsest Phantom (Boss): Ghostly manuscript that periodically phases out of reality.
  • THE OBLIVION LEXICON (Final Boss): Multi-phase flying grimoire of absolute bibliographic erasure!
