Codex Mnemosyne

The Marginalia War — An Illuminated Chronicle of the Sunken Scriptorium

Beneath a tideless leaden sea stands the Clockwork Abbey of Saint Jude of the Lost Syllable. Within its brass-ribbed scriptorium rests the Codex Mnemosyne—the last surviving illuminated manuscript containing humanity's fragile daydreams, half-remembered lullabies, the scent of petrichor, and the names of childhood imaginary friends.

Now, an entropic contagion known as The Unwriting (the Censorship of Oblivion) has infected the leather binding. From the Torn Spine on the margin, living inkblots, bureaucratic redaction monoliths, weeping errata wraiths, and hungry paper-mites march along the calligraphic ribbon toward the Heart-Glyph at the bottom of the page. Should they devour the Heart-Glyph, the memories inscribed upon this folio will bleach into blank, meaningless parchment forever.

You are the Master Rubricator. Dipping your stylus in Gilded Ink, you must sketch living medieval-surrealist automata—brass quill-ballistas, vermilion snail-mortars, harmonic seraphim, chrono-sand hourglasses, and prismatic astrolabes—along the margins. Between every wave of the Unwriting, the Scriptorium enters a state of stillness, allowing you to illuminate four sacred tiers of upgrades upon your creations.
