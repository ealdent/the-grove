Field Notes of Jean-Luc Vaneau (1888)

"They told me at the Guild that scent is transient — that once the stopper is pulled, memory disperses into the ceiling plaster and is gone forever. They are blind fools. Smell is the only bridge to the primordial soul."

The Discovery of the Septième Distillat

In November of 1888, sixty fathoms beneath the limestone vaults of Grasse, my alembics condensed the Seventh Essence. It radiates the soft warmth of childhood dawn: sunlit linen, rain on warm stone, and orange blossom honey. But its distillation created an ethereal fracture. The Abyssal Malodors — ancient spirits of sour tallow, stagnant bile, and corrosive industrial vapor — smell its purity and crawl toward the Grand Flacon.

The Five Distillation Stills

The Bergamot Pipette: Rapidly drops acidic citrus darts. Upgrades grant needle piercing, armor dissolution, and tri-burst solar ignitions.

The Lavender Cryo-Condenser: Emits frosty mist coils that chill approaching vapors. High tiers flash-freeze enemies into shattering ice.

The Ambergris Mortar: Catapults heavy blobs of sticky sperm-whale resin that detonate into burning, slowing craters.

The Clove-Spark Coil: Discharges crackling arcs of eugenol bio-electricity that leap between multiple targets with micro-stuns.

The Rose-Attar Prism: Ramps an unbroken beam of thermal Damascena ruby light that incinerates heavy monstrosities.
