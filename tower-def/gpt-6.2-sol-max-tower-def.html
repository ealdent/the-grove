Municipal Weather Vault

Field guide

Sixty-three years ago the Dry Act sent every inconvenient forecast to the vault. Streets stayed open. Garden plots became parking lots. Children learned thunder from library recordings.

Night custodian Iona Vale found a note in the file for the last rain cloud. The writer had hoped her daughter would live long enough to stand in the rain. Tonight Iona has propped open the release gate. Hold the Skylatch through twenty fronts while its pressure equalizes.

Working the chart

  • Choose an instrument, then place it in a dotted socket beside the drainage channel. The circle shows its reach.
  • During an intermission, select an installed instrument to upgrade or salvage it. Each has four upgrades. Clearing a front pays a stipend plus any brass the weather leaves behind.
  • Watch the forecast before releasing a front. Weather that reaches the Skylatch breaks seals. When all seals break, the vault floods.
  • Use 1–5 to choose an instrument. Arrow keys move between sockets and Enter places or inspects. N releases, pauses, or resumes a front. Esc clears your selection.
