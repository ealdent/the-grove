Tidepool Tactics

✦ Defenders of the Droplet ✦
One drop of ocean. A thousand tiny teeth. Hold the roe until the tide turns.
🌊 Read the tide. The gauge on the right rises & falls on a ~30s cycle. At HIGH TIDE enemies swim faster and splash/chain towers reach farther. At LOW TIDE enemies crawl exposed, take drying damage, and your Sun income & sun-towers surge. Every tide flip fires a free synchronized volley!
🛡 Play: Tap a hero in the tray, then tap open water beside the silt current to place it. Tap a placed hero to upgrade (4 tiers) or sell. Press 🐚 Send Wave when you're ready — sending early pays bonus Sun.
🥚 Don't let the marauders reach the amber roe at the current's end. Survive all 24 waves and the eggs hatch free.
