📖 The Archivist's Codex: Anno 1889

The Heresy of the Unbound Margin

"For three hundred years, the Grand Clockwork Scriptorium of Prague has transcribed the Prime Ledger—the mechanical manuscript whose brass gears and silver-nitrate ink keep causal history running on time. Tonight, Apprentice Vance knocked over a vial of alchemical Vermilion Solvent. Now, the typos have grown legs."
— Master Artificer Erasmus Vane, Chief Censor

Every blot, censored phrase, and forgotten footnote ever struck from the Ledger has coalesced into The Errata. Crawling from the Inkwell Spill on the Left Margin, they follow the conductive silver-nitrate capillary groove toward the Mainspring Colophon on the Right Margin. Should twenty Errata slip inside the Colophon's escapement gears, reality itself will be misprinted.
