Station Log — KVLT

Emberwick, the cliff. Night of the long static.

They call this stretch of the dial the Dead Band, and they are right to. Every night since the mast went up in '31, this station has sung the Sleepwave across the valley — the one frequency that keeps Emberwick dreaming. The town has never known. The town must never know.

Because when the transmitter falls silent, the Hollow Hours come through. Things of pure static — Hisses, Crackles, Whistlers, Deadairs — crawling up the signal cable to smother our voice. The Operator before me learned this too slowly. I found only his headphones, still warm.

Tonight the ionosphere broke. The Hollow Hours are already on the cable, wave upon wave, and dawn is twenty-four watches away. My apparatus is old, my valves are warm, and my hand is steady.

I am the night operator. As long as KVLT broadcasts, Emberwick sleeps.

— final entry, station logbook · 1937
