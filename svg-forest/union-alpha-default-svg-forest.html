Field manual / no. 071

It rained upward.
Then the city followed.

On a Tuesday nobody remembers, every raindrop changed its mind. The gutters rose first. Then the roofs. By evening, the city was a small dark shape beyond the sun.

Only the Department of Unsent Weather stayed behind. Its copper umbrellas still grow. Its paper skates still migrate. And inside its barometer houses, five forecasts wait for a day that never came.
