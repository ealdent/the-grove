The Seventh Autumn

WINTERCOMB

The Hived Sun

When the early Frost cracked the sky’s ecliptic, the Keepers opened every skep in the valley. For forty days the Great Swarm climbed the cold air and drank the failing October sun drop by drop—carrying afternoon down into monumental combs of beeswax.

Now the world endures a hundred-year dusk, warmed only by stored noon. As the Night Apiarist, wander the frost-heather meadows among Propolis Lantern-Oaks, brass Smoker-Censers, and slow glass-winged Sun-Bees—and keep the old custom of Telling the Bees at each sleeping straw skep so the swarm remembers morning.
