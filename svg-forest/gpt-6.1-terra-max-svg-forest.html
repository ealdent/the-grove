When the constellations lost their names, their letters fell out of the sky. The Night Post built towers to keep them safe, but the postmasters have long since gone.

You are the last keeper. Wander the quiet flats, follow the paper moths, and walk close to a glowing post tower to read what the stars left behind. There is no end to the road.
