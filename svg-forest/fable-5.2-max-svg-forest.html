Shade is water here.

The sea that died on this plain left its salt and waited. Then someone raised a stone to count the hours, and the sea came up into its shadow: a hand deep at noon, a canal by evening, an ocean by night.

The dial-keepers are gone, and you have a pair of their stilts. Walk with the shade. Where a lily opens under it, stand close. That is how an hour is kept.
