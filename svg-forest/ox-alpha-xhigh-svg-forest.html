EMBERFALL

The Cinder Terraces

Long after the forge‑star fell, its cooling shell stretches past every horizon. Basalt hardened into hollow flute‑stones that vent the last warmth; shards of obsidian mirror the ember sky; fumaroles breathe over glowing cracks; and cinder moths — sparks that learned to fly — drift on the heat. Walk as long as you wish. The terraces do not end. They only remember.
