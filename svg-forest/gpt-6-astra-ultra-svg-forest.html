Tidekeeper's notebook

What the water kept.

When the last ocean withdrew, its keepers planted its sounds in porcelain trees. Every hollow bell holds one small thing the water once knew: a harbor, a whale's turning, a child testing the cold with one foot. The keepers have been gone for centuries. Their trees still grow across the salt, and folded creatures tend the branches beneath a sun that never quite sets.

You are the newest tidekeeper. Follow your listening needle and linger beside six ringing trees. Beyond each remembered ocean, another orchard waits, pale and unfinished, without an opposite shore.
